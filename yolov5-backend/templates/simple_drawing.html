YOLO Camera - Simple Drawing Interface

How to Use:

  1. Select a shape: Click on one of the shape buttons below
  2. Draw on video: Click and drag directly on the video stream to draw shapes
  3. Shape buttons on video: You can also click the shape buttons that appear on the video stream
  4. Manage shapes: Use the action buttons to undo or clear shapes
  5. Detection: When a person enters any drawn shape, an alert will be triggered

YOLO Camera with Interactive Drawing

How to Use the Drawing Interface:

  1. Select a shape: Click on one of the shape buttons (Rectangle, Triangle, Polygon, Circle)
  2. Draw shapes: Click and drag on the video to draw the selected shape
  3. Multiple shapes: You can draw multiple shapes by selecting different shape types
  4. Undo: Click "Undo Last Shape" to remove the most recent shape
  5. Clear All: Click "Clear All Shapes" to remove all drawn shapes
  6. Detection: When a person enters any drawn shape, an alert will be triggered
